# User experience

Authenticated users have a personal dashboard, presentation preferences, saved searches, favorites, recent-page shortcuts, private drafts, notifications, and personal access tokens.

- [Dashboard](dashboard.md)
- [Settings and preferences](settings.md)

Generated static sites do not have accounts or account-backed features. Their read-only presentation preferences are stored in the browser.

## Offline pages

Previously visited pages can remain available offline in supported browsers. Offline copies are tied to the signed-in account, and signing out or switching accounts clears them.

Reconnect before relying on offline content after restarting the browser. Offline pages are snapshots and may be outdated until Kumbuka can connect to the server again.
